# Iowa Lead(II) Azide POGIL

A Science 9 guided-inquiry case study for STEM Innovation Academy. Students follow the lead from the November 25, 2026 destruction of lead(II) azide at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant: nomenclature, conservation of mass, environmental transport, hazard versus risk, monitoring data and exposure standards.

Live: https://stem-ia.github.io/iowa-lead-azide/

`index.html` is one self-contained file. It makes no network requests, sets no cookies and needs no login. Links to CCOHS, OSHA, EPA, the Army and the Missouri government open in a new tab.

What stays on the device: student responses save in that browser's local storage only. Nothing is sent anywhere. Work leaves the device only when the student exports a text file or prints.

The Model 5 monitoring data are simulated and labelled as such.

Built by Ben Wood, Digital Learning Coordinator, STEM Innovation Academy.
